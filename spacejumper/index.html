Privacy Policy for Space Jumper

Effective date: 19 November 2026

This privacy policy describes how the Space Jumper mobile game ("the app"), (package com.saccej.umpeerr), published by NK MobileApps, handles information.

Data collection and sharing

The app does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. It does not collect or share any data with third parties.

The app contains no advertising, no analytics or tracking SDKs, no account registration, and no in-app purchases. Game progress and settings are stored only locally on your device and are never sent off the device.

Permissions

The app requests only network-status permissions (internet access, network state, Wi-Fi state). It does not access your location, contacts, photos, files, microphone, or camera.

Children

Because the app collects no data at all, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children of any age.

Changes to this policy

If the way the app handles information changes, we may update this policy and change the date shown above.
