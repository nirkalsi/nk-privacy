AI Tools Compared - Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026. This policy applies to the Android app "AI Tools Compared" (package nir.com.ailearning) published by NK MobileApps.

What the app does

The app shows learning lessons and quizzes. Your lesson progress and quiz results are stored only on your device. We do not run user accounts and we do not receive your progress.

Information we collect

We do not collect your name, email, location or contacts. The app uses Google AdMob to show ads, and Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and use device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), IP address and app interaction data to serve and measure ads, to prevent fraud and to improve its services. Where required, the app asks for your consent through Google's consent form.

Advertising

Ads are provided by Google AdMob. You can reset or opt out of personalised ads in your device settings (Settings > Google > Ads). See Google's policy: policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Data sharing and retention

We do not sell personal data. Data processed by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is handled by Google under its own privacy policy. Local progress data is deleted when you clear the app data or uninstall the app.

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above.
