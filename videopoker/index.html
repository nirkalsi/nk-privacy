Video Poker - Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026. This policy applies to the Android game "Video Poker" (package nir.com.blackjack21) published by NK MobileApps.

What the game does

The game is a free social slot-machine game for adults. All coins are virtual, free, and have no cash value; the game does not offer real-money gambling and you cannot win real money or prizes. Your coin balance and progress are stored only on your device. We do not run user accounts and we do not receive your progress.

Information we collect

We do not collect your name, email, location or contacts. The game uses Google AdMob to show ads, and the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and use device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), IP address and app interaction data to serve and measure ads, to prevent fraud and to improve its services. Where required, the game asks for your consent through Google's consent form.

Advertising

Ads are provided by Google AdMob. You can reset or opt out of personalised ads in your device settings (Settings > Google > Ads). See Google's policy: policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Adults only

The game is intended for players aged 18 and over and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Data sharing and retention

We do not sell personal data. Data processed by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is handled by Google under its own privacy policy. Local game data is deleted when you clear the app data or uninstall the game.

Data deletion requests

To request deletion of any personal data associated with Video Poker: Casino Cards, email kalsinir@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion request" and we will delete the data within 30 days. Local game data is deleted immediately when you clear the app data or uninstall the game. Advertising data processed by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is handled by Google and can be reset or deleted through your device ad settings. We do not keep any additional personal data after a deletion request is completed.
