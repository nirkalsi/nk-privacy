Velvet Spotlight Privacy Policy

NK MobileApps. Android package: nir.com.velvetspotlight. Last updated: 26 November 2026.

Velvet Spotlight is a simulated slot and theatre-building game for adults aged 18 and over. Virtual coins and rewards have no cash value. They cannot be withdrawn, transferred or redeemed for money or real-world prizes. The game does not provide real-money gambling.

Free play and your device

You can use the free game without a Velvet Spotlight account. Free progress, free simulated coins, settings and reward receipts are stored on your device. Free progress is separate from the online purchased-coin balance. Clearing app storage or uninstalling can remove local progress. Free progress is not recovered through the online purchased-coin account. Android app backup is disabled for these saves.

Optional Google sign-in and purchased coins

If you choose the online purchased-coin features, you sign in with Google through Firebase Authentication. This creates or signs in to a Velvet Spotlight game account. Google and Firebase process identity information, including account identifiers, email address and profile information supplied by Google, along with authentication and security information such as IP addresses and user-agent information.

The app sends an identity token to our backend over HTTPS to verify the game account and current sign-in status. The backend uses account-linked identifiers to connect the game identity to its purchased-coin balance. These identifiers are pseudonymous, not anonymous. Free play remains available without this sign-in.

Google Play handles payment and asks you to confirm a purchase. The game backend receives a purchase token and verifies it with Google Play before granting purchased coins. It records the product, quantity, coin amount, purchase-token hash, account binding, verification and consumption status, and related timestamps. Google handles your payment details. Do not send card details or sign-in secrets to support.

The online service records the purchased-coin balance and paid-round requests, results, payouts and account revisions to handle retries and prevent duplicate debits or credits. A refused insufficient-funds request may be recorded so that retrying it after a later top-up does not create an unexpected debit. Network interruptions can leave a request pending until its status can be checked.

Refunds and cancellations can reverse the related purchased-coin credit. Reversing already spent credit can adjust the available paid balance. A negative virtual balance is not a monetary debt and does not create a new payment charge. Google Play's separate refund rules apply. Free coins and purchased coins are separate balances.

Providers and purposes

Google and Firebase provide sign-in and authentication security. Google Play provides billing, purchase verification, refunds and purchase-consumption services. Cloudflare hosts the backend and game-data storage. These services process data to provide the requested features, maintain account and transaction consistency, prevent abuse, and handle verified support and deletion requests. Support correspondence sent to the contact address is processed through the email service used for that mailbox.

Advertising and privacy choices

Velvet Spotlight includes Google's AdMob Mobile Ads SDK and User Messaging Platform. Current versions can show banner ads and optional rewarded ads. Ad requests are made only when the consent state reported by Google's User Messaging Platform allows them. Watching a rewarded ad is optional. The advertising SDK and its startup component remain part of the app.

Google's advertising SDK may collect and share IP addresses, which can indicate approximate location; app and ad interactions; diagnostic and performance information; advertising ID, app set ID and other applicable device or account identifiers. Google uses this information for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. These advertising services do not themselves create a Velvet Spotlight game account.

The app does not request precise location, contacts, camera or microphone access for these features. No separate developer analytics service is included. Google states that Mobile Ads data is encrypted in transit using TLS. Read Google's Privacy Policy and advertising information, and manage relevant advertising settings in Android and your Google Account. Where available, use Privacy choices in the game's Rules & settings screen to revisit consent choices.

Retention, requests and backups

Active account, purchased-coin and transaction records are kept while needed to provide and recover the online features, verify transactions and address support, refund, security or fraud issues. Pending purchase-consumption retry material is stored encrypted and subject to the service's retry window. Removing retry material does not itself confirm consumption or a refund.

Request records contain the identifiers needed to match the request to the game account, the request reference, status and relevant times. Identity details in the request queue are encrypted. Temporary verification challenges and abuse-prevention counters expire and are removed through scheduled maintenance. Support correspondence is used to verify and handle the request and explain its status.

Deleting active data does not instantly remove every provider backup, restore-safety record or support message. Minimal account-linked safety records may remain where needed to prevent deleted data from reappearing after a restore or to finish outstanding security, fraud, support or refund work. These records are not described as anonymous. We review retained records and explain justified retention when handling a deletion request; we do not promise that every copy is removed immediately.

Firebase states that Authentication logged IP addresses remain for a few weeks, and that other authentication information is removed from live and backup systems within 180 days after deletion is initiated. Provider retention is separate from removal of active game records.

Requesting deletion

Request deletion of your Velvet Spotlight game account and associated game data through the Request deletion of game account link in the paid-feature area, through the web resource below without reinstalling the game, or by contacting kalsinirch@gmail.com.
https://velvet-purchases-staging.kalsinirch.workers.dev/account-deletion/request

We verify that the request relates to your game account before taking action. The secure form verifies the existing Google-linked game account and asks for a second explicit confirmation. It does not create an account if none exists. Sending a request is not confirmation of deletion. We review requests and explain their status, any necessary further steps and justified retention. Do not send passwords, verification codes, authentication tokens or full payment details by email.

Deleting the game account does not delete your Google account, delete other apps or automatically issue a refund. Local free-game data on installed devices is separate; clear app storage to remove it. Purchased coins cannot be recovered after the associated account and records are deleted. Advertising data independently processed by Google is subject to Google's policies and controls. We do not claim that clearing local storage deletes data independently processed by Google.
