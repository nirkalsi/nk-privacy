Velvet Spotlight Privacy Policy

NK MobileApps · Android package: nir.com.velvetspotlight
Last updated: November 21, 2026

This policy describes the first, accountless version of Velvet Spotlight, a simulated slot and theatre-building game. Coins and rewards have no cash value and cannot be redeemed or transferred for money or real-world prizes.

Game progress stays on your device

This version does not offer account registration, Google sign-in, coin purchases or a cloud wallet. Your game progress, simulated coin balance, settings and reward receipts are stored locally on your device. The app does not upload these game saves to a game account server or provide cross-device progress recovery. Android app backup is disabled for these saves.

Advertising and consent

The app uses Google's AdMob Mobile Ads SDK to provide a bottom banner and optional rewarded video ads. Watching a rewarded ad is your choice. The app uses Google's User Messaging Platform to request privacy consent where required and to offer privacy options when Google requires them. Ad requests are gated on the consent state reported by that platform.

Google's advertising SDK may collect and share your IP address (which can indicate approximate location), app and ad interactions such as app launches, taps and video views, diagnostic and performance information, Android advertising ID, app set ID and other applicable device or account identifiers. Google uses this information for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. This does not create a Velvet Spotlight player account.

The app does not request precise location, contacts, camera or microphone access. No separate developer analytics service is included in this version. This does not mean advertising services collect no information. Google states that data transmitted by its Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit using TLS.

Read Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information for advertising. You can also manage relevant advertising settings in Android and your Google Account. Where available, use Privacy choices in the game's Rules & settings screen to revisit consent choices.

Deleting data

There is no Velvet Spotlight online player account to delete in this version. To remove the app's local game saves and settings, use Android Settings to clear the app's storage or uninstall the app. Clearing storage or uninstalling removes your local progress, and this version has no cloud recovery.

Advertising data processed by Google is subject to Google's policies and controls. For questions about data associated with your use of this app, contact us at the address below. Do not send passwords, sign-in tokens or payment card details. We do not claim that clearing local storage deletes data independently processed by Google.

Audience

This simulated casino game is intended for adults aged 18 and over, not children. It does not provide real-money gambling, cash prizes or cashouts.
